Clean Agent Suppression (Novec 1230 vs FM-200) for Data Centres

Home / Blog / Article

Water-based sprinkler systems are essential for structural building protection, but they cannot be deployed inside server halls, battery energy storage rooms (BESS), or high-voltage motor control centers (MCC) without destroying sensitive electronics. In these mission-critical enclosures, gaseous Clean Agent Total Flooding Suppression Systems provide zero-residue fire extinguishment.

The two most widely deployed clean agents are Novec 1230 (FK-5-1-12) and FM-200 (HFC-227ea), engineered strictly to NFPA 2001 standards. Understanding their chemical differences, environmental lifespans, and storage footprints is critical for facility designers.

1. Novec 1230 (FK-5-1-12): The Eco-Friendly Chemical Leader

Novec 1230 (Fluoroketone) is a high-tech fluid that is stored as a liquid at room temperature and super-pressurized with nitrogen at 25 or 42 bar. Upon nozzle discharge, it instantly vaporizes into a gas, extinguishing combustion primarily through thermal heat absorption:

  • Environmental Profile: Zero Ozone Depletion Potential (ODP) and an ultra-low Global Warming Potential (GWP) of less than 1, with an atmospheric lifetime of just 5 days. It is completely exempt from global HFC phase-downs.
  • Human Safety: Extremely high safety margin (67 to 139 percent) between design concentration (typically 4.5 to 5.9 percent) and the No Observable Adverse Effect Level (NOAEL).

2. FM-200 (HFC-227ea): Proven Legacy Suppression

FM-200 (Heptafluoropropane) is a hydrofluorocarbon gas that extinguishes fire by disrupting the chemical chain reaction of combustion. Key characteristics include:

  • Performance: Extinguishes fires in 10 seconds or less with zero residue, protecting live computing hardware.
  • Environmental Considerations: While zero ODP, FM-200 has a Global Warming Potential (GWP) of 3,220 and is subject to progressive phase-down quotas under the Kigali Amendment.

Engineering Recommendation

For new data center installations, server rooms, and battery storage enclosures, Novec 1230 is the recommended industry choice due to its future-proof environmental sustainability, large safety margin for occupied rooms, and superior total flooding performance.
