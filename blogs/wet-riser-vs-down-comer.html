Wet Riser vs Down-Comer Hydrant Systems: Which Does Your Facility Need?

Home / Blog / Article

When engineering an internal fire suppression network for multi-story industrial facilities, warehouses, or manufacturing plants, selecting the appropriate vertical piping delivery system is critical. The National Building Code of India (NBC 2016 Part 4) and IS 3844 define two primary vertical piping architectures: the Wet Riser System and the Down-Comer System.

While both systems supply high-pressure water to landing valves and first-aid hose reels across multiple building floors, their mechanical design, pressure maintenance, and pump configurations differ fundamentally.

1. The Wet Riser System: Permanent High-Pressure Readiness

A wet riser is an internal vertical pipeline permanently charged with water under constant, high pressure directly from a ground-level pump house and static water reservoir. Key characteristics include:

  • Continuous Pressurization: The entire vertical pipe column is pressurized (typically at 7.0 to 8.5 bar) via an automated jockey pump and main fire pumps located in a ground-level pump room.
  • Instant High-Volume Delivery: Opening any landing valve on any floor immediately discharges high-velocity water streams, triggering automatic fire pump engagement within seconds.
  • Building Application: Mandatory under NBC 2016 for industrial buildings exceeding 15 meters in height, high-density warehousing, and all moderate to high-hazard manufacturing complexes.

2. The Down-Comer System: Gravity & Terrace Booster Supply

A down-comer system is a vertical pipe connected directly to an overhead terrace water tank, relying on gravity flow or a small terrace-mounted booster pump to deliver water downwards to landing valves. Key characteristics include:

  • Overhead Water Source: Water is supplied from a dedicated terrace storage tank (typically 10,000 to 25,000 liters) located on the building roof.
  • Terrace Booster Pump: A small electric pump (typically 450 to 900 LPM at 2.0 to 3.5 bar head) installed at the terrace level pressurizes the downward flow when a hose reel is opened.
  • Building Application: Permitted only in low-rise commercial structures and low-hazard industrial buildings under 15 meters in height where ground pump room footprint is strictly constrained.

Key Technical Comparison

Feature Wet Riser System Down-Comer System
Water Source Ground Static Reservoir (1,00,000L+) Overhead Terrace Tank (10,000L - 25,000L)
Pump Location Ground-Level Pump Room (Main Electric + Diesel) Terrace Booster Pump Only
Operating Pressure 7.0 to 10.5 bar constant 2.0 to 3.5 bar variable
Height Threshold All structures, mandatory >15 meters Low-rise only (<15 meters)

Which System Does Your Industrial Facility Require?

For modern manufacturing plants, logistics warehouses, and multi-tier chemical facilities, a Wet Riser System is almost universally mandatory under NBC 2016 Part 4. Its high-capacity water reserves, diesel engine backup redundancy, and superior pressure delivery ensure adequate fire suppression during severe emergencies.
