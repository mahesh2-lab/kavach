Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC)

A multi-million-rupee fire protection system is absolutely worthless if it fails to activate during an emergency. Severe industrial environments cause rapid equipment degradation—steel pipes rust, hydraulic valves seize, detector chambers fill with microscopic dust, and backup batteries slowly die. Kavach provides exhaustively documented Comprehensive and Non-Comprehensive Annual Maintenance Contracts specifically designed to guarantee your life-safety infrastructure remains 100% operational, 365 days a year.

Rigorous Preventative Maintenance Schedule

We do not simply wipe down panels and tick boxes. Kavach's AMC protocols mandate aggressive, invasive testing of every single component in your fire network, adhering strictly to the maintenance guidelines set forth in IS 2189 (Alarms) and IS 13039 (Hydrants).

  • Weekly / Monthly Micro-Testing

    Essential preventative checks. We conduct automated start-logic testing of massive diesel and electrical fire pumps, perform specific gravity and voltage checks on backup battery banks, visually inspect all hydrant landing valves for leaks, and verify the main control panel is free of systemic fault codes.

  • Quarterly Deep Servicing

    Aggressive mechanical maintenance. This includes physically dismantling and cleaning smoke detector optical chambers, injecting high-grade industrial grease into seized hydrant valves, fully unrolling and pressure-testing 30-meter canvas fire hoses, and simulating live alarm logic to verify HVAC shutdown relays.

  • Annual System Load Testing & Certification

    The ultimate stress test. We conduct full sustained load testing of your pump house, utilize calibrated flow meters to verify exact GPM delivery at the hydraulically most remote yard hydrants, perform hydro-static testing on extinguisher cylinders, and issue the mandatory 'Form-B' Certificate of Maintenance required for your NOC renewal.

Frequently Asked Questions

Authoritative engineering guidance on statutory standards, system design parameters, and mandatory maintenance schedules.

What specific testing protocols are covered during quarterly fire AMC visits?
Quarterly AMC servicing includes automatic pump start tests, diesel engine battery and fuel line checks, and pressure relief valve calibrations. Technicians also conduct hydrant landing valve flushing, alarm panel loop diagnostic scans, and sprinkler flow switch testing.
What is the distinction between preventive and corrective fire protection maintenance?
Preventive maintenance involves scheduled routine inspections, lubrication, and adjustments designed to prevent component degradation before failures occur. Corrective maintenance encompasses emergency repairs, valve overhauls, and replacement of damaged hardware following an unexpected breakdown.
What is the legal meaning and importance of Form A and Form B certifications in Maharashtra?
Form A is the initial certificate issued by a licensed agency certifying that newly installed fire systems conform strictly to government norms. Form B is the mandatory bi-annual certificate issued every January and July confirming that existing fire systems remain fully operational.
What typical breakdown response time SLAs are guaranteed under an industrial fire AMC?
Kavach guarantees a 2-hour on-site breakdown response SLA across major Pune industrial belts including Chakan, Bhosari, Talegaon, and Ranjangaon. Critical emergency support lines remain operational 24 hours a day, 365 days a year for urgent pump room outages.
What equipment and services are included versus excluded in a standard industrial fire AMC?
Standard comprehensive AMCs include routine maintenance labor, scheduled servicing visits, compliance documentation, and emergency breakdown attendance. Major equipment replacements, capital pipeline modifications, and structural civil works are handled under transparent pre-agreed terms.
How is diesel engine pump reliability guaranteed during prolonged electrical power failures?
Diesel fire pumps are tested under weekly automated cranking schedules with dual battery bank health checks and trickle charger verifications. Fuel tank levels, cooling water heat exchangers, and exhaust back-pressure points are inspected to guarantee instant start-up during blackouts.
Why is non-destructive thickness testing (NDT) conducted on aging fire hydrant pipelines?
Ultrasonic thickness testing measures remaining pipe wall thickness to detect internal pitting corrosion without shutting down plant operations. Identifying thin pipe sections early prevents high-pressure pipe ruptures during emergency fire pumping operations.
What digitized documentation records are provided to facility managers after each AMC service?
Facility managers receive timestamped digital service reports containing pressure test logs, detector loop resistance values, and photo documentation of inspected valves. These electronic records streamline third-party safety audits and statutory annual insurance inspections.
