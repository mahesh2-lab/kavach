Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC)

A multi-million-rupee fire protection system is absolutely worthless if it fails to activate during an emergency. Severe industrial environments cause rapid equipment degradation—steel pipes rust, hydraulic valves seize, detector chambers fill with microscopic dust, and backup batteries slowly die. Kavach provides exhaustively documented Comprehensive and Non-Comprehensive Annual Maintenance Contracts specifically designed to guarantee your life-safety infrastructure remains 100% operational, 365 days a year.

Rigorous Preventative Maintenance Schedule

We do not simply wipe down panels and tick boxes. Kavach's AMC protocols mandate aggressive, invasive testing of every single component in your fire network, adhering strictly to the maintenance guidelines set forth in IS 2189 (Alarms) and IS 13039 (Hydrants).

  • Weekly / Monthly Micro-Testing

    Essential preventative checks. We conduct automated start-logic testing of massive diesel and electrical fire pumps, perform specific gravity and voltage checks on backup battery banks, visually inspect all hydrant landing valves for leaks, and verify the main control panel is free of systemic fault codes.

  • Quarterly Deep Servicing

    Aggressive mechanical maintenance. This includes physically dismantling and cleaning smoke detector optical chambers, injecting high-grade industrial grease into seized hydrant valves, fully unrolling and pressure-testing 30-meter canvas fire hoses, and simulating live alarm logic to verify HVAC shutdown relays.

  • Annual System Load Testing & Certification

    The ultimate stress test. We conduct full sustained load testing of your pump house, utilize calibrated flow meters to verify exact GPM delivery at the hydraulically most remote yard hydrants, perform hydro-static testing on extinguisher cylinders, and issue the mandatory 'Form-B' Certificate of Maintenance required for your NOC renewal.

Why Kavach AMC?

  • 24/7 Priority Emergency Response: We guarantee a maximum 4-hour on-site deployment of senior engineers for critical system breakdowns (alarms constantly ringing, pump failures) within the extended industrial regions.
  • Dedicated Safety Account Manager: You receive a direct line to a senior fire engineer, eliminating frustrating call-center queues and ensuring streamlined, highly technical communication.
  • Massive Spare Parts Inventory: We maintain a huge local inventory of genuine OEM parts (addressable detectors, deluge valves, pump impellers) allowing for immediate, same-day replacements to brutally minimize system downtime.

Contract Structures

Comprehensive AMC: Ultimate peace of mind. Includes the total cost of all preventative labor, emergency visits, AND the cost of all replacement spare parts and extinguisher refilling throughout the year.

Non-Comprehensive AMC: Highly cost-effective. Covers all scheduled preventative maintenance labor and testing. Replacement parts and refilling chemicals are quoted and billed separately at actuals only when required.
