End-to-End Industrial Solutions

Comprehensive Fire Protection Engineering

At Kavach Fire Safety Industrial Services, we engineer, supply, rigidly install, and meticulously maintain state-of-the-art fire safety systems designed specifically to combat severe industrial and commercial hazards. Every system we design is strictly compliant with the National Building Code (NBC) of India, Tariff Advisory Committee (TAC) guidelines, and international NFPA standards, ensuring you receive maximum protection and guaranteed regulatory approval.

01

Fire Hydrant Systems

The absolute backbone of industrial fire defense. We engineer high-pressure wet riser networks capable of delivering massive volumes of water across sprawling facilities. Our comprehensive turnkey service includes advanced pump room design—integrating electric main pumps, heavy-duty diesel engines, and precision jockey pumps to maintain constant network pressure under TAC guidelines.

  • Heavy-duty external Class-C GI ring mains
  • Internal landing valves & strategic hose reels
  • Dedicated Fire Brigade inlet connections
  • Automated pump control panels

02

Fire Alarm & Detection

Early detection is the single most critical factor in life safety. We deploy highly intelligent, microprocessor-based addressable life-safety networks providing instantaneous hazard identification. These advanced multi-sensor arrays boast zero false-alarm rates and seamlessly integrate with building management systems to trigger automated HVAC shutdowns, elevator grounding, and phased evacuations.

  • Analog addressable multi-sensor networks
  • Aspiration smoke detection (VESDA) for IT
  • Linear heat sensing cables for conveyors
  • BMS & Public Address (PA) integration

Automated Sprinklers

Automated suppression arrays that provide immediate, localized fire control directly at the ignition source. We perform extremely complex hydraulic calculations to determine the exact K-factors, pipe sizing, and water density requirements necessary for your specific hazard classification, ensuring optimal thermal knockdown capabilities before the fire brigade even arrives.

  • Conventional Wet & Dry pipe systems

  • High-velocity Deluge systems for transformers
  • ESFR In-rack warehouse sprinklers

Extinguisher Lifecycle

The crucial first line of defense for incipient stage fires. We provide complete risk-based mapping, supply, installation, and scheduled hydro-testing of portable and heavy-duty trolley-mounted fire suppression units. Every unit we supply is strictly BIS (IS:15683) marked, ensuring reliability when you need it the absolute most.

  • ABC Dry Chemical Powder (Multi-purpose)
  • Carbon Dioxide (CO2) for electrical panels

  • Clean Agents (Novec 1230 / FM-200)
