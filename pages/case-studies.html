Logistics & Warehousing

Securing a 4,00,000 Sq.Ft E-Commerce Fulfillment Hub

The Critical Challenge

A massive logistics warehouse in Bhiwandi faced an immediate Fire NOC rejection from the local authorities. The facility utilized dense, 18-meter high-rack storage configurations. Traditional ceiling sprinklers were deemed completely inadequate because the immense rack height would obstruct water flow to the lower tiers, creating vertical fire tunnels. Operations were halted, causing severe financial hemorrhaging.

The Kavach Solution

Our engineering team was deployed within 24 hours. We completely redesigned the hydraulic calculations and proposed a sophisticated 3-Tier In-Rack ESFR (Early Suppression Fast Response) Sprinkler Grid. We bypassed the roof obstruction by threading the sprinkler piping directly through the storage racks. Additionally, we installed Reflective Beam Smoke Detectors across the vast ceiling spans, calibrated to ignore forklift exhaust while instantly detecting combustion smoke.

The Outcome

The entire high-hazard installation was successfully completed and pressure-tested within 45 days. The facility achieved rigorous NBC Part-4 compliance, securing their Final Fire NOC ahead of schedule and allowing the client to safely commence e-commerce operations.

Petrochemical Processing

Emergency Overhaul of a Volatile Chemical Plant

The Critical Challenge

An aging solvent manufacturing facility in the MIDC zone was flagged during a surprise Factory Inspectorate audit. The underground yard hydrant ring was heavily corroded, leaking pressure constantly, and the primary 150HP diesel pump was mechanically seized. The plant was operating at a severe risk of a catastrophic Class B (flammable liquid) fire and faced immediate operational shutdown.

The Kavach Solution

Shutting down the plant for a month was financially impossible for the client. Kavach executed a highly phased, hot-work replacement strategy. We replaced 2,000 meters of corroded underground piping with Heavy Class GI wrapped in anti-corrosive tape—while maintaining partial system pressure. We installed a brand new Kirloskar diesel pump set, integrated Mechanical Foam (AFFF) monitors near the solvent tanks, and replaced all electrical panels with Flameproof (Explosion-Proof) enclosures.

The Outcome

The facility successfully passed the stringent Factory Inspector re-audit with zero compliance deviations. The plant is now covered by a Kavach Comprehensive AMC, operating safely at 100% capacity with zero recorded downtime.
