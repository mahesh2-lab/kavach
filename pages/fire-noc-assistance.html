Fire NOC & Liaison Assistance

Acquiring a No Objection Certificate (NOC) from the Fire Department is not just about installing equipment—it is a stringent, highly documented, legally binding bureaucratic process. Without a valid NOC, you cannot legally occupy your building, commence manufacturing operations, or secure commercial insurance. Kavach acts as your dedicated regulatory bridge, providing end-to-end liaison and technical engineering support to guide you through the complex maze of the Maharashtra Fire Services and MIDC authorities.

The Complete Fire NOC Lifecycle

As an approved, licensed agency, we handle everything from the initial architectural CAD drawings to the final hand-shake with the Chief Fire Officer. We specialize in rapidly clearing bureaucratic bottlenecks for complex industrial projects.

1
Provisional NOC (Pre-Construction)

Before laying a single brick, we review your architectural layouts and engineer a comprehensive fire safety blueprint (zoning, tank capacities, pump curves). We submit this technical dossier to the CFO to secure the Provisional NOC required to commence building construction legally.

2
System Execution & Form-A Generation

Our execution teams install the hydrants, sprinklers, and alarms exactly as per the CFO-approved drawings. Upon rigorous testing and commissioning, Kavach issues the legally required 'Form-A' (Certificate of Installation), verifying the system is built to code.

3
Final NOC (Occupancy Certificate Stage)

We coordinate and manage the final physical inspection by Fire Department officials. Our engineers are present on-site to successfully demonstrate the functional readiness of all pumps, alarms, and hydrants to secure your Final Fire NOC, unlocking your Occupancy Certificate (OC).

4
Annual Renewal & Form-B (Jan/July)

NOCs are not permanent; they must be renewed half-yearly (in January and July) under the Maharashtra Fire Prevention Act. We conduct the statutory maintenance checks and issue 'Form-B' (Certificate of Maintenance) to keep your compliance flawlessly updated.

Technical Documentation We Handle

The CFO requires an exhaustive dossier of technical and legal documents. We compile, verify, and submit the entire package:

  • Approved Architectural & Elevation Plans (AutoCAD)
  • Detailed Block Plan highlighting 6-meter fire tender access paths
  • Comprehensive Fire Safety System Engineering Drawings
  • Signed Structural Stability Certificates
  • Previous MIDC / Municipal Corporation Approvals
  • Electrical Inspectorate Certificates

Kavach holds the required government licenses to officially sign and issue Form-A and Form-B certificates, drastically accelerating the approval process.
