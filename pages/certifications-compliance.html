Certifications & Compliance

Fire safety in India is heavily regulated by statutory law. Non-compliance is not merely a financial or operational risk—it is a severe criminal liability under the law. At Kavach, we don't just install pipes and alarms; we engineer legal peace of mind. Every system we design, install, and maintain strictly adheres to the highest national and international safety codes, ensuring your facility is unconditionally compliant with local fire authorities, factory inspectorates, and insurance mandates.

The Statutory Framework

Operating an industrial or commercial facility without an active, certified fire suppression system is illegal under the Maharashtra Fire Prevention and Life Safety Measures Act, 2006. Failure to produce a valid Fire NOC (No Objection Certificate) or Form B compliance certificate can result in immediate sealing of premises, disconnection of water and electricity, and imprisonment of the facility's occupiers.

Insurance Implications

In the event of a fire, insurance surveyors immediately request the latest Form B certificate and system maintenance logs. Without strict adherence to Tariff Advisory Committee (TAC) guidelines, insurance claims running into crores can be instantly repudiated.

NBC 2016 Compliant

All our system architectures rigidly adhere to the National Building Code of India (Part 4: Fire and Life Safety). We precisely calculate static water tank capacities, diesel pump head curves, down-comer sizing, and evacuation stairwell pressurization according to your building's specific hazard classification.

BIS / ISI Standards

We refuse to compromise on material integrity. We source and install only equipment bearing the Bureau of Indian Standards (ISI) mark. This includes IS:15105 for sprinkler heads, IS:2189 for fire alarm control panels, IS:15683 for portable fire extinguishers, and IS:1239 for heavy-class GI piping.

NFPA / TAC / FM Global

For multinational corporations, heavy industries, and exceptionally high-risk facilities, we have the engineering capability to design systems that meet or exceed stringent international standards, including NFPA (National Fire Protection Association), FM Global, and Indian Tariff Advisory Committee (TAC) guidelines.
