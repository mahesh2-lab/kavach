Specialized Sprinkler Topologies

Wet Pipe Systems

The industry standard. The piping network is constantly pressurized with water. When localized ambient heat bursts the specific temperature-rated glass bulb of an individual sprinkler head, water discharges instantaneously. Extremely reliable; ideal for offices, retail malls, and heated manufacturing plants.

Pre-Action Systems

Engineered for environments where accidental water discharge equals catastrophic financial ruin (e.g., IT Data Centers, Server Farms, Museum Vaults). Requires a two-step validation: an independent smoke alarm must first trigger to flood the dry pipes with water, and then localized heat must burst the sprinkler bulb to release it.

High-Velocity Deluge Systems

Deployed in extreme high-hazard zones like chemical processing plants, fuel loading bays, and massive electrical transformers. All sprinkler heads remain permanently open. When a fire alarm triggers, a massive deluge valve opens, instantaneously flooding the entire protection zone with immense volumes of water simultaneously.

Hydraulic Design & Installation

Our engineering process guarantees that the water density perfectly matches the fuel load of your facility:

  • Hazard Classification Matrix: We exhaustively categorize your site as Light, Ordinary, or Extra High Hazard to determine the absolute required Water Density (LPM/sq.m) and Area of Operation.
  • Software Hydraulic Calculations: Utilizing advanced CAD engineering software, we calculate precise friction loss through every meter of the piping network, ensuring even the hydraulically most remote sprinkler head receives optimal operating pressure.
  • ESFR In-Rack Sprinklers: For massive logistics warehouses with storage racks exceeding 12 meters, standard ceiling sprinklers are useless. We design sophisticated In-Rack sprinkler grids that bypass shelf obstruction, deploying water directly into the heart of the stacked cargo.
  • Execution & Commissioning: Flawless pipe threading, grooved jointing, and rigorous hydro-testing of the entire array to 1.5x working pressure to absolutely guarantee zero leakage.
