Industrial Fire Safety Training

The most advanced, expensive fire suppression system in the world is absolutely useless if your staff panics, fails to activate it, or mishandles the equipment during an emergency. Human intervention during the first 3 critical minutes of a fire determines whether you experience a minor incident or a total catastrophic loss. Kavach conducts rigorous, hands-on Industrial Safety and Fire Fighting training programs designed to transform your ordinary employees into a capable, legally compliant Emergency Response Team (ERT).

Comprehensive Training Modules

Our training goes far beyond boring classroom lectures. We conduct dynamic, live-action training sessions directly on your factory floor, utilizing controlled live-fire pans and high-pressure water systems to simulate real-world stress scenarios.

  • Extinguisher Handling (PASS Method)

    Fundamental chemistry of the Fire Triangle. Trainees learn to instantly identify fire classes (A: Wood, B: Oil, C: Gas, D: Metal) and select the correct extinguisher type (DCP, CO2, Foam). Crucially, every participant practices the hands-on PASS method (Pull, Aim, Squeeze, Sweep) on actual live flames.

  • Evacuation & Mock Drills

    We coordinate massive, full-facility surprise evacuation drills. We train designated Floor Wardens on crowd control psychology, anti-stampede tactics, conducting accurate assembly point headcounts, and safely evacuating physically challenged personnel through dense smoke scenarios.

  • Advanced Hydrant Operation for ERT

    Specialized, high-intensity training exclusively for your Emergency Response Team (ERT) and security guards. Hands-on practice in unrolling 30-meter canvas hoses, operating heavy hydrant landing valves, and safely managing the extreme recoil of high-pressure water jets without injury.

Legal Compliance & Certification

Under Section 38 of the Maharashtra Fire Prevention & Life Safety Measures Act and the Factories Act (1948), conducting bi-annual mock drills and maintaining a certified ERT is an absolute legal mandate for industrial facilities.

Upon successful completion of the training module, Kavach issues a formal Certificate of Training. This document provides legal validation that your staff has undergone authorized safety instruction, a crucial requirement during stringent Factory Inspector audits.
