Request a Site Inspection

Don't wait for a compliance notice or an emergency. Let our certified fire safety engineers conduct a comprehensive preliminary assessment of your facility to identify critical vulnerabilities and regulatory gaps.

What to Expect During the Inspection

Our preliminary site inspection is a crucial first step in securing your facility. A senior engineer from Kavach will visit your premises to evaluate your current safety posture against current National Building Code (NBC) guidelines.

1
Initial Briefing

We meet with your facility managers to understand the operational nature of your business, hazardous materials on-site, and previous fire safety history.

2
Physical Walkthrough

Our engineers conduct a physical survey of your manufacturing floor, warehouses, electrical panels, and server rooms to identify fire loads and ignition risks.

3
System Evaluation

If you have existing systems, we perform a visual check of hydrant pressure gauges, alarm panel health, extinguisher expiry dates, and sprinkler head integrity.

4
Actionable Reporting

Within 48 hours of the inspection, you will receive a high-level gap analysis report detailing urgent vulnerabilities, compliance shortfalls, and an estimated quotation for remediation.
