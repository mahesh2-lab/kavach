The Critical First Line of Defense

Industrial Fire Extinguishers & Lifecycle Services

Portable fire extinguishers are undeniably the most critical tools for suppressing incipient-stage fires before they can inflict catastrophic structural or operational damage. However, an extinguisher is only effective if it is the correct type for the specific hazard, strategically placed for immediate access, and rigorously maintained to guarantee instantaneous operation. Kavach provides complete, end-to-end extinguisher lifecycle management—from facility risk mapping and initial supply to certified BIS-standard refilling and hydro-testing.

ABC Dry Chemical Powder

The highly versatile workhorse of industrial safety. Utilizing Monoammonium Phosphate, these units form a barrier between oxygen and the fuel source. Ideal for suppressing Class A (solid combustibles), Class B (flammable liquids), and Class C (electrical) fires. Indispensable for massive warehouses, general manufacturing floors, and corporate office spaces.

Capacities: 2kg, 4kg, 6kg, 9kg, 50kg Trolley

Carbon Dioxide (CO2)

Discharges a freezing cloud of CO2 gas that completely displaces ambient oxygen, suffocating the fire while leaving absolute zero residue behind. The ultimate, non-destructive choice for critical IT server rooms, massive electrical panels, and highly sensitive CNC manufacturing equipment where chemical powder cleanup would be financially disastrous.

Capacities: 2kg, 3kg, 4.5kg, 9kg, 22.5kg Trolley

Mechanical Foam (AFFF)

Specifically engineered to combat highly volatile Class B (flammable liquid) fires. The Aqueous Film-Forming Foam rapidly blankets the liquid fuel surface, simultaneously cutting off oxygen, cooling the thermal layer, and physically suppressing vapor release to entirely prevent explosive re-ignition. Perfect for chemical storage and heavy fuel depots.

Capacities: 9L, 50L Trolley

Clean Agents (Novec / FM200)

Advanced, eco-friendly, non-toxic gas extinguishers that disrupt the actual chemical chain reaction of a fire on a molecular level. Highly specialized for exceptionally critical environments such as advanced data centers, centralized automated control rooms, and sterile pharmaceutical laboratories where both fire and water damage are unacceptable.

Capacities: 2kg, 4kg, 5kg

Hazard Mapping & Rigid Maintenance

Purchasing extinguishers is merely the first step; deploying them correctly is what saves lives. Kavach engineers conduct rigorous on-site hazard mapping based strictly on NBC Part 4 and IS 2190 guidelines. We mathematically determine the exact quantity, correct fire-rating, and precise spatial distribution of extinguishers required to protect your specific operational zones.

Furthermore, a neglected extinguisher is significantly more dangerous than no extinguisher at all. Kavach operates a fully certified, state-of-the-art refilling workshop dedicated to ensuring your equipment functions flawlessly when a crisis hits.

Comprehensive IS:2190 Servicing Protocols

  • Mandatory Annual Inspections

    Exhaustive physical checks including gross weight verification, pressure gauge validation, hose integrity testing, and anti-tamper seal verification.

  • Certified Media Refilling

    Complete evacuation of old suppressants, thorough internal cylinder inspection for corrosion, and precise refilling utilizing only high-grade, BIS-certified extinguishing media.

  • Hydro-Static Pressure Testing (HPT)

    Subjecting the steel cylinder to extreme water pressure tests every 3 to 5 years to absolutely guarantee structural integrity and prevent fatal cylinder explosions during use.
