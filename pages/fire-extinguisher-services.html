Industrial Fire Extinguishers & Lifecycle Services

Portable fire extinguishers are undeniably the most critical tools for suppressing incipient-stage fires before they can inflict catastrophic structural or operational damage. However, an extinguisher is only effective if it is the correct type for the specific hazard, strategically placed for immediate access, and rigorously maintained to guarantee instantaneous operation. Kavach provides complete, end-to-end extinguisher lifecycle management—from facility risk mapping and initial supply to certified BIS-standard refilling and hydro-testing.

ABC Dry Chemical Powder

The highly versatile workhorse of industrial safety. Utilizing Monoammonium Phosphate, these units form a barrier between oxygen and the fuel source. Ideal for suppressing Class A (solid combustibles), Class B (flammable liquids), and Class C (electrical) fires. Indispensable for massive warehouses, general manufacturing floors, and corporate office spaces.

Capacities: 2kg, 4kg, 6kg, 9kg, 50kg Trolley

Carbon Dioxide (CO2)

Discharges a freezing cloud of CO2 gas that completely displaces ambient oxygen, suffocating the fire while leaving absolute zero residue behind. The ultimate, non-destructive choice for critical IT server rooms, massive electrical panels, and highly sensitive CNC manufacturing equipment where chemical powder cleanup would be financially disastrous.

Capacities: 2kg, 3kg, 4.5kg, 9kg, 22.5kg Trolley

Mechanical Foam (AFFF)

Specifically engineered to combat highly volatile Class B (flammable liquid) fires. The Aqueous Film-Forming Foam rapidly blankets the liquid fuel surface, simultaneously cutting off oxygen, cooling the thermal layer, and physically suppressing vapor release to entirely prevent explosive re-ignition. Perfect for chemical storage and heavy fuel depots.

Capacities: 9L, 50L Trolley

Clean Agents (Novec / FM200)

Advanced, eco-friendly, non-toxic gas extinguishers that disrupt the actual chemical chain reaction of a fire on a molecular level. Highly specialized for exceptionally critical environments such as advanced data centers, centralized automated control rooms, and sterile pharmaceutical laboratories where both fire and water damage are unacceptable.

Capacities: 2kg, 4kg, 5kg

Engineering Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Authoritative engineering guidance on statutory standards, system design parameters, and mandatory maintenance schedules.

What do the IS 15683 marking requirements signify on industrial fire extinguishers?
IS 15683 markings certify that the extinguisher has passed standardized BIS performance, burst pressure, and fire rating tests for Classes A, B, C, D, and F. Cylinders must display embossed manufacturing dates, test pressures, and holographic BIS certification labels.
How should industrial fire extinguisher classes be selected by hazard type?
Class A extinguishers handle solid combustibles like wood and paper, Class B targets flammable liquids, Class C treats energized electrical equipment, and Class D tackles combustible metals. Selecting the proper extinguishing medium prevents dangerous chemical reactions and shock hazards.
What is the mandatory refilling and hydrostatic pressure testing frequency for extinguishers?
Water, foam, and dry chemical powder extinguishers require refilling and servicing every year, with mandatory hydrostatic pressure testing conducted every three years. Carbon dioxide (CO2) cylinders must undergo statutory hydraulic stretch testing every five years.
Why is clean agent gas preferred over CO2 inside sensitive electrical control panels?
Clean agents such as Novec 1230 and FM-200 extinguish fires chemically without leaving residue or inducing severe thermal shock on hot electronic components. Carbon dioxide can cause rapid cold-shock cracking to delicate circuit boards and presents human asphyxiation risks.
When are trolley-mounted fire extinguishers required instead of portable handheld units?
Trolley-mounted 25kg to 50kg extinguishers are required in high-risk zones such as fuel unloading gantries, paint booths, and chemical solvent stores. Their higher capacity and continuous discharge rate provide sustained firefighting suppression before external emergency teams arrive.
What mounting height and signage rules apply to factory fire extinguishers under IS 2190?
Extinguishers weighing under 4kg must be mounted so the top handle is no higher than 1.5 meters from finished floor level, while heavier units must be positioned at 1 meter. Prominent high-visibility photoluminescent identification signs must be placed above every extinguisher station.
How is ABC dry chemical powder tested for purity and moisture contamination?
ABC powder must contain a minimum of 50 percent monoammonium phosphate content verified through laboratory chemical titration. Moisture contamination tests verify that powder remains free-flowing and does not cake inside the cylinder during prolonged storage.
What maintenance log documentation is required for industrial fire extinguisher audits?
Facility managers must maintain an inspection register recording individual cylinder serial numbers, location barcodes, monthly weight checks, and hydro-test dates. This register forms a core component of the mandatory bi-annual Form B certification submitted to local fire authorities.
