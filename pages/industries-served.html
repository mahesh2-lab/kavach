Industries We Protect

Fire risks are strictly non-universal. A petrochemical plant handling volatile solvents faces entirely different thermal hazards and ignition sources than a multi-story commercial IT park. Deploying a generic fire system is a recipe for disaster. At Kavach, we engineer bespoke fire protection infrastructures mathematically tailored to the specific chemical, structural, and operational vulnerabilities of your distinct industrial sector.

Heavy Manufacturing & Auto

Extreme high-heat environments burdened with combustible raw materials, CNC machining oils, heavy electrical robotics, and constant welding operations. A fire here means total supply chain collapse.

Key Solutions:

  • High-capacity automated hydrant rings
  • High-velocity deluge water spray arrays
  • Spark detection for dust extraction ducts
  • Strict Factories Act (1948) Compliance

Warehouses & Logistics Hubs

Massive floor plates with 15-meter high-rack storage. Pallets of cardboard and plastics create vertical fire tunnels. Traditional ceiling sprinklers are completely ineffective at penetrating the lower racks.

Key Solutions:

  • Multi-tier In-Rack Sprinkler systems
  • Early Suppression Fast Response (ESFR)
  • Reflective beam smoke detectors
  • High-volume diesel fire pump houses

Chemicals & Pharmaceuticals

Highly volatile environments prone to rapid Class B (flammable liquid) fires and explosive vapor ignition. Standard water suppression can actually spread chemical fires.

Key Solutions:

  • Mechanical Foam (AFFF) suppression systems
  • Explosion-proof (Flameproof) detectors
  • Clean agent gas suppression for control rooms
  • Intrinsic safety circuit design

IT Parks & Data Centers

High-occupancy multi-story buildings housing vast, critical IT infrastructures. Accidental water discharge from sprinklers can cause more financial damage than a small fire.

Key Solutions:

  • Pre-Action dry pipe sprinkler networks
  • Novec 1230 / FM200 Server Room flooding
  • Intelligent Addressable Alarm & PA Integration
  • NBC Part-4 High-Rise Evacuation Compliance

Hospitals & Healthcare

The most complex evacuation scenarios. Patients in ICUs or operating theaters are non-ambulatory. Fires must be contained aggressively to allow horizontal evacuation.

Key Solutions:

  • Highly zoned wet-pipe sprinkler systems
  • Automated smoke extraction fan integration
  • Oxygen line cutoff relays
