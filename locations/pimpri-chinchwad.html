1. Industrial Character & Hazard Density

The Pimpri-Chinchwad (PCMC) industrial cluster, centered around Bhosari MIDC, represents one of Asia’s densest manufacturing belts. Housing thousands of machine tool units, sheet metal fabrication shops, precision tooling works, and chemical ancillary facilities, PCMC industrial plants require retrofitting obsolete fire panels, pump room overhauls, and strict municipal fire compliance.

Operating industrial assets in this corridor requires strict compliance with the Maharashtra Fire Prevention and Life Safety Measures Act 2006, the Factories Act 1948, and NBC 2016 Part 4. Kavach Fire Safety provides dedicated engineering support tailored to the specific operational hazards of plants in this region.

2. Statutory Compliance & MIDC Liaison Services

Navigating municipal fire department approvals and MIDC Chief Fire Officer (CFO) requirements requires local technical expertise. As a state-certified Licensed Agency, Kavach manages the full compliance lifecycle for facilities in this zone:

  • Provisional & Final Fire NOC: Preparation of architectural CAD fire blueprints, static water calculations, and direct representation during official CFO site inspections.
  • Bi-Annual Form B Renewals: Mandatory physical maintenance audits conducted every January and July to maintain unbroken statutory legal standing.
  • Third-Party Safety Audits: Comprehensive risk assessments under IS 14489 and Section 38 of the Factories Act 1948.

3. Turnkey Fire Protection Engineering & 24/7 AMC

Kavach maintains rapid-response mobile engineering teams dedicated to this industrial sector. We provide complete turnkey design, procurement, installation, and maintenance across critical suppression systems:

  • High-Pressure Hydrant Networks: IS 13039 external yard hydrants, landing valves, and pump rooms with electric, diesel standby, and jockey pumps.
  • Automatic Sprinkler Systems: Ceiling ESFR sprinklers for high-rack warehouses and pre-action systems for server rooms.
  • Addressable Fire Alarms: Early smoke detection loops, VESDA air sampling, and automated HVAC/Access Control interlocks conforming to IS 2189.
  • Guaranteed Emergency SLA: On-site emergency breakdown attendance within 2 hours to resolve pump faults, false alarms, and valve impairments.
